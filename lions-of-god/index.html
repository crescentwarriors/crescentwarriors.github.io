The Game

Fight the great battles of early Islamic history as the Companions — the Lions of God. Stand as Hamza ibn Abdul-Muttalib, Khalid ibn al-Walid, Amr ibn al-As and more of the heroes of the first generation, may Allah be pleased with them all — one warrior against entire armies.

Lions of God is a campaign that spans the age of the Companions — from the Battle of Badr and Uhud, through the Ridda wars, to the great openings of Persia and Rome, and the opening of Al-Quds in the time of Umar ibn al-Khattab, may Allah be pleased with them all. Every hero fights in his own style and walks his own path through history. Built in Unreal Engine 5. Development begins with the Battle of Badr.

Questions

What is Lions of God?

A large-scale action game of the great battles of early Islamic history, built in Unreal Engine 5 by Crescent Warriors Studio. You fight as the Companions, may Allah be pleased with them all — the Lions of God — one hero against entire armies, across a campaign that spans the age of the Companions. Development begins with the Battle of Badr.

Which heroes can I play?

Hamza ibn Abdul-Muttalib — Asadullah — is the first. Khalid ibn al-Walid, Amr ibn al-As and more of the Companions follow, may Allah be pleased with them all, each with his own weapons, his own fighting style and his own arc of battles through history.

Which battles are in the game?

The campaign spans the age of the Companions, may Allah be pleased with them all: from the Battle of Badr, through the Ridda wars, to the great openings of Persia and Rome and the opening of Al-Quds in the time of Umar ibn al-Khattab. Development begins with the Battle of Badr.

What platforms will it be on, and when?

The goal is a cross-platform release: PC, PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo Switch. The game is in development and there is no release date yet. Subscribe on YouTube or join the email list to follow every milestone.

Is the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ depicted in the game?

No. In keeping with Islamic tradition, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is never depicted, and the heroes of early Islamic history are portrayed with care and reverence, grounded in the historical sources.
