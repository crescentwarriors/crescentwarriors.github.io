The Game

One warrior against a thousand. Stand as Hamza ibn Abdul-Muttalib — Asadullah, the Lion of God — on the sands of Badr, where 313 believers faced an army three times their size.

Lions of God is a campaign of the defining battles of early Islamic history, built in Unreal Engine 5. Development begins where legends were forged — the Battle of Badr: capture the wells, answer the duel, hold the line, and turn the day.

Forged From History

Fight as the Lion

Fluid one-versus-many combat: combos, duels, and the Sawlah — an unleashed charge that turns the tide of battle.

A True Battlefield

Hundreds of warriors clash on screen at once. Capture wells, protect the camp, and face the commanders of Quraysh.

Told With Respect

Grounded in the historical sources, with Arabic voice acting and an unwavering commitment to authenticity and reverence.

From the Battlefield

The Vision

Lions of God portrays the heroes of early Islamic history with care and reverence. In keeping with Islamic tradition, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is never depicted.
